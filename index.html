HOW I WORK

Make the complexity clear.

I don’t start with screens. I start by understanding what the system needs to do, who needs to do it, what rules constrain it, and where things can go wrong.

01

Requirement

Understand business goals, constraints, roles, and requirements.

02

Business Logic

Translate rules, permissions, states, and exceptions into a usable model.

03

Workflow

Map the journeys and handoffs between people and system states.

04

UX / UI

Turn complexity into clear information architecture and interfaces.

05

Handoff

Document decisions, components, states, and interactions for development.

06

UAT

Validate the product against real workflows and edge cases.
